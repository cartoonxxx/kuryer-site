Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age to work as a courier?

18 years old — all year round. In summer, from June to late August, Yandex and X5 annually hire students from 16: a written consent from a parent or guardian and a birth certificate are required.

How much does a courier earn in 2026?

Monthly benchmarks: on foot — from 45,000 to 75,000 RUB depending on the service, by car — up to 170,000–270,000 RUB. Depends on the city and the service.

Can I work as a courier without experience?

Yes. No experience needed: the app builds your route, and your first day is on-the-job training with live orders and tips. You will need: age 18+, a Russian passport or EAEU/RVP/VNZh documents, and a smartphone. A thermal bag is provided free of charge.

Courier work near home — how do I choose my area?

In Yandex Eda and Magnit Delivery you pick your zone when signing up — choose areas near your home. At Lavka you work in the darkstore's neighborhood. You can change your zone in the app.

What documents does a courier need?

A Russian passport, or documents from EAEU countries (Kazakhstan, Armenia, Kyrgyzstan, Belarus), a temporary residence permit (RVP), a residence permit (VNZh), or a work permit (patent). Holders of a work patent cannot work in Saint Petersburg.

When do payouts arrive?

Yandex Eda and Lavka — daily for self-employed couriers. Kuper — Friday; with a high rating — Monday. Magnit Delivery and X5 — Thursday for the previous week.

How do I register as self-employed?

In 10 minutes via the 'Moy Nalog' app — no sole-proprietor registration or cash register needed. Tax is 4% on income from individuals and 6% from companies. The income cap is 2.4 million RUB a year — courier earnings will not reach it.

Can a 16-year-old work as a courier?

Yes, in summer. From June to late August, Yandex and X5 annually hire students from 16: written parental or guardian consent and a birth certificate.

Can I combine courier work with my main job?

Yes, self-employment does not conflict with an employment contract, and you do not need to notify your employer. The only limit is if a non-compete clause is explicitly written into your contract.
