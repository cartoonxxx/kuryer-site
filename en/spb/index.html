Who can work as a courier in SPb

  • RF citizens — all 5 services, no restrictions;
  • Citizens of the EAEU (Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan) — work on the same terms as Russians, no patent needed;
  • Holders of a temporary or permanent residence permit — work on general terms, the ban does not apply to them;
  • Patent holders — not allowed: since 22 November 2025, Saint Petersburg has banned courier and food delivery work under a patent (governor's decree), and the ban runs through 2026. Alternative — warehouse roles: storekeeper, order picker.

FAQ about courier work in SPb

How much does a courier earn in Saint Petersburg?

On foot — 60 000–90 000 ₽ a month at 8 hours and 5 days a week. On a bicycle or e-bike — 115 000–175 000 ₽ at full load. In your own car — 160 000–220 000 ₽. The calculator above shows exact figures for your schedule.

Can you work as a courier without experience?

Yes, no experience is needed in any of the 5 services. Your first shift comes with a mentor, or you get short in-app training. What matters is a phone with a decent camera and a willingness to meet deadlines.

Does a courier in SPb need a health book?

For grocery delivery (Magnit, X5, Lavka, Kuper) — yes. A personal health book is issued in SPb in 1–2 days, and many services reimburse its cost once you start working shifts. For restaurant delivery via Eda, a health book is usually not required.

Can you work as a courier in SPb with a patent?

No. Since 22 November 2025, a governor's decree in Saint Petersburg has barred foreign citizens working under a patent from courier and food delivery — the ban runs through 2026. Citizens of the EAEU (Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan) and foreigners with a residence permit can work — they do not work under a patent. Services offer couriers with a patent alternative warehouse roles: storekeeper or order picker.

How often do payouts come?

Yandex Eda and Lavka — daily on request. Kuper — after each shift. Magnit and X5 — on Thursdays. The money goes to your card; self-employment or partner-company status is arranged when you sign up.

Do you need your own transport?

For on-foot delivery — no. For bicycle couriers at Eda, an e-bike is available for 1 ₽ a day when you complete 70 orders a week. Car couriers need their own car and a category B licence.

What is a scheduled slot?

It is a pre-booked work window, for example 10:00 to 14:00. A scheduled slot has a minimum hourly rate — you get paid even when there are few orders. A free slot means going out 'whenever you want', with no minimum.

Can you combine two services?

Yes, many couriers work for two services: scheduled slots in one and free slots in the other. Nothing in the terms of the services prevents working for several delivery services at the same time.

How long does registration take?

From application to the first shift — 1–3 days. Online registration in the app takes 20–30 minutes, then an interview with the partner company and issue of gear.

Is courier work available for students?

Yes, evening and weekend slots are a standard option for students in SPb. The 4-hour minimum slot allows 1–2 shifts a day between classes.

Income figures are estimates based on public vacancies; actual payouts depend on the service, district, slot times and activity. This page is not an offer.
