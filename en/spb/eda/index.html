Slots: scheduled and free-form

Scheduled slot

Next week's booking opens on weekends — queue order follows your rating. Hourly minimum pay applies even without orders. Cancel no later than 12:00 the day before; being over 30 minutes late counts as a no-show.

Free slot

Start and finish without a booking — a side-gig format: from 1 hour and 1 order. Come online whenever you want; income follows completed orders.

Rating

Slot types affect your rating. High rating means earlier and better slots and more quality, well-paid orders. Auto-accept kicks in after 2–3 ignored orders — watch your status so you don't get unfavorable orders.

Important for Saint Petersburg

Since late 2025, couriers with a work patent cannot work in Saint Petersburg — the restriction runs through 2026 and covers the entire courier sector, including food delivery. However, EAEU citizens (Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan) and holders of RVP and VNZh work as usual. RF citizens can choose either format: self-employed status or a GPH contract.

Bonuses and goals

Newcomers get up to ₽15,000 for ~100 orders in 14 days (terms vary by month). Promo bonuses appear too: deliver 128 orders between the 1st and 6th of the month and earn ₽9,500. In peak autumn and winter seasons daily goals appear, e.g. 30 orders a day for ₽3,200.

Payouts

Self-employed: daily (orders from Friday to Sunday arrive on Monday). Contract via a park: every two weeks, schedule depends on the park. Bonuses and tips — on Wednesdays.

Equipment

Pick up your thermal bag at a Market pickup point or the courier center — it stays yours.

Where orders come from and where pay is higher

Orders come from restaurants, bakeries, pharmacies, grocery stores, Ozon and Yandex Market. The Yandex Pro map highlights high-demand zones — go there and earn noticeably more via surcharges and multipliers.

How to take your first slot

  1. Register: RF citizens online, CIS citizens at the courier center.
  2. Install the Yandex Pro app and complete a short tutorial.
  3. Pick up your thermal bag at a Market pickup point or the courier center.
  4. Pick a short 4–6 hour slot in a familiar area — start with scheduled slots, they carry hourly minimum pay.
  5. Before going online: charged phone and power bank, mobile internet, weather-appropriate clothes, water.

A workday walkthrough

Open the app, sign in, start the slot. An order arrives — the route is built automatically in Yandex Pro. Tap that you've arrived, collect the ready order or wait for it to be packed. Routes can change mid-ride as new orders arrive. The courier sees the building entrance, floor, apartment and the client's contact number in the app. Support is always online and ready to help with any issue. Greet, hand over the order, wish them a nice meal, tap 'delivered'. Politeness directly affects tips. Then wait for the next order and repeat.

Operator call after registration

After you submit the form, an operator will explain work and pay terms, give income figures, confirm your documents and transport type, and invite you to the courier center for onboarding.

FAQ

How much does a Yandex Eda courier earn in Saint Petersburg?

On foot: from ₽60,000 a month at full load; e-bike: ₽120,000–180,000; car: ₽160,000–250,000. Depends on slots, demand zones and bonuses.

How do I take my first slot?

Slots tab in Yandex Pro → a short 4–6 hour scheduled slot in a familiar area → 'Start slot'. Always start with scheduled slots — they carry hourly minimum pay.

When do payouts arrive?

Self-employed: daily. Park contract: every two weeks. Bonuses and tips — Wednesdays.

Can I work via self-employment or a GPH contract while officially employed?

Yes. Self-employment (SMZ) is compatible with official employment. A GPH contract via a park can also be arranged alongside your main job.

What if I can't make my scheduled slot?

Cancel by 12:00 the day before — then it counts as a proper cancellation. Being over 30 minutes late equals a no-show.

Can I work with a work patent?

Not in Saint Petersburg: patent holders are barred from courier work through 2026. EAEU citizens, RVP and VNZh holders work as usual — confirm your onboarding format with the operator.

What documents do I need to register?

RF citizens need a passport. GPH requires passport, SNILS and INN. CIS citizens register only via GPH — passport and migration documents, full-package onboarding at the courier center. Format options: self-employed status — registration in the 'My Tax' app takes about 10 minutes, daily payouts; GPH — the park issues the contract, payout terms are discussed separately, payment to a third-party card is possible.

Can I combine it with studies or another job?

Yes: a free slot needs just 1 hour and 1 order — go online whenever you want. Add scheduled slots in peak hours for stable income.

Do I need a health certificate?

A health certificate is listed among requirements, but in practice it's not always requested right away. Check with the operator after registration.
