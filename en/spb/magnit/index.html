Slots

Scheduled slots only, 4 to 12 hours

There is no free-form slot function. Slots are booked in advance in the Magnit Dostavka app: open the Schedule section, pick a day and time, a map circle with a number shows how many slots are free at that time, then take a slot, choose your starting store and confirm. You work from one to three stores within your chosen zone — territory or district. Zones cover all districts of the city, from the center to the outskirts; the app shows the exact territories and starting stores.

Important for Saint Petersburg

Since late 2025, couriers with a patent are not allowed to work in Saint Petersburg — the restriction runs for the whole of 2026 and covers the entire courier industry, delivery included. Citizens of the EAEU (Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan), as well as holders of RVP and VNZh, work as usual.

Rating

The rating only affects long-term slot planning. A low rating unlocks slots 3 days ahead. A high rating — 7 days ahead, at better times with peak hours. The rating never resets to zero: it changes when you are late, leave a slot early, or commit similar violations.

Bonuses and goals

Personal goals appear almost every week — though not in every city. Example: deliver 40 orders by the deadline without breaking the service rules and earn ₽10,000. Rules for the goal period: no more than 10 order rejections and no more than one early departure from a scheduled slot. Goals are modest but frequent — they pair well with regular shifts. Newcomers get ₽10,000 for 100 orders in 10 days.

Transport and fuel

Couriers work on foot, by bike, e-bike and car. Car couriers get fuel reimbursement — ask the operator for the amounts and rules.

Payouts

Self-employed: every Thursday for the previous week, via Qugo or Konsol.Pro. On payout day you must sign the settlement act, otherwise the money will not be released — amounts may arrive in parts. Via a park under GPH: every two weeks, the schedule depends on the park — confirm during onboarding. Paying to a third-party card is not allowed.

Courier center

A visit to the Magnit courier center is required before you start. RF citizens need a minimum set of documents — passport, INN, SNILS. CIS citizens need the full document package, onboarding goes through the courier center. Gear and a thermal bag are issued there. Your account is activated only after the visit. The operator will give you the address of the courier center in Saint Petersburg after registration.

Equipment

Gear and thermal bag are issued at the courier center during onboarding. A Saint Petersburg note: frequent wind and rain — a rain cape will come in handy.

Where orders come from

Orders come from Magnit stores: groceries and household goods. In peak hours and demand zones there are more orders — take evening slots.

How to start

  1. Sign up via the link below — an operator will call you with the terms.
  2. Visit the Magnit courier center with your documents: RF citizens need a minimum set, CIS citizens — the full package. Gear and a thermal bag are issued there.
  3. Once your account is activated, install the Magnit Dostavka app and complete a short tutorial.
  4. Book your first scheduled slot of 4 hours or more in the Schedule section.
  5. Before going online: charged phone and power bank, mobile internet, weather-appropriate clothes, water.

Operator call after registration

After you submit the form, an operator will explain work and pay terms, give income figures, confirm your documents and transport type, and invite you to the courier center for onboarding.

A day at work

Open the app, take a selfie and a photo of your bag — if everything checks out, your slot has started. Head to the store where your slot is booked. An order comes in: enter the store, go to the checkout, politely ask where pickups are, give the order number and PIN code. Got the order — put it in your bag. Open the Yandex map inside the Magnit app, the route is built automatically. On arrival, tap that you are at the location. The app shows the entrance number, intercom, floor and apartment. Greet the customer, hand over the package, wish them a nice meal, say goodbye and mark the order as delivered. Head back to the store and wait — or pick up a new order on the way. And so on, in a loop, for the whole slot.

FAQ

How much does a Magnit courier earn in Saint Petersburg?

At full load (7 days, 12 hours): bike up to ~₽109,000 a month, car up to ~₽164,000. Car couriers get fuel reimbursement, plus personal goals and bonuses.

How do slots work?

Scheduled slots only, from 4 hours. There is no free-form slot function. Booked in advance in the Magnit Dostavka app.

Can I take short slots?

Yes, scheduled slots can be 4 hours long — in the morning or evening, easy to combine with a job or studies. An evening short slot usually earns more than a morning one: the evening is peak demand time. With a high rating you can see the schedule a week ahead, so short slots can be planned around your free days.

What if I cannot make it to a scheduled slot?

Cancel the slot in the app no later than 12 hours before the start — then it counts as a regular cancellation. The rating drops by 1 point but never resets. A low rating means slots open only 3 days ahead without peak hours; a high rating gives 7 days and better times.

When do payouts arrive?

Self-employed: every Thursday for the previous week. Via a park: the schedule follows the park's contract — confirm during onboarding. Paying to a third-party card is not allowed.

What transport do Magnit couriers use?

On foot, by bike, e-bike, and car. Car couriers get fuel reimbursement.

Does Magnit Dostavka work at night?

No, Magnit has no night slots — work runs in daytime and evening hours only. If you are looking for night side jobs, this service will not fit; on the other hand, short evening slots cover the demand peak.

Can I work via self-employment or a GPH contract while officially employed?

Yes. Self-employed status is compatible with official employment. A GPH contract via a park also works alongside a main job. Paying to a third-party card is not allowed in Magnit.

What documents do I need to register?

RF citizens need a passport, INN and SNILS. CIS citizens bring a passport and migration documents and onboard via a park. A courier-center visit is mandatory — gear and thermal bag are issued there.

Can I work as a Magnit courier in Saint Petersburg with a patent?

No: since late 2025 couriers with a patent are not allowed to work in Saint Petersburg — the restriction runs for the whole of 2026. Citizens of the EAEU (Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan), as well as holders of RVP and VNZh, work as usual — apply via the courier center.

Do I need a health certificate?

A health certificate is listed among requirements, but in practice it's not always requested right away. Check with the operator after registration.
