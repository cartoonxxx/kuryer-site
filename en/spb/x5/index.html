Slots: scheduled and free (exchange)

Scheduled slot

Book a shift in advance in the X5 app — fixed work time. Booking format and schedules depend on the zone; the operator will give details after registration.

Free slot (exchange)

Available to RF citizens. Go online anytime without booking, wait for a convenient order, skip inconvenient ones — no rating loss or penalties. Done for the day — stop the search and head home.

Important for Saint Petersburg

Since late 2025, couriers with a work patent cannot work in Saint Petersburg — the restriction runs through 2026 and covers the entire courier sector, including delivery. However, EAEU citizens (Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan) and holders of RVP and VNZh work as usual.

Bonuses and goals

Personal goals appear regularly: e.g. complete 240 orders by the deadline and earn ₽17,000. Amounts and terms depend on the city and month. Newcomers get a welcome bonus that varies by city. The rolling promo «Impuls» runs regularly — check the app for details.

Payouts

Self-employed: every Thursday after 12:00 for the previous week, paid via T-Bank; if it doesn't arrive, a backup lands on Tuesday. GPH: every two weeks, schedule depends on the park.

Equipment

Bag and gear are issued either at the dark store or at the courier center, depending on the location — ask the operator during onboarding.

Where orders come from

Orders come from X5 stores: Pyaterochka, Perekrestok and Chizhik. Peak hours bring more orders — schedule morning and evening shifts.

How to start

  1. Fill in the form: RF citizens may register online; CIS citizens may need a courier-center visit.
  2. Install the X5 app and complete a short tutorial.
  3. Take your first scheduled slot or go online on the exchange.
  4. Pick up your bag and gear — at the dark store or the courier center.
  5. Before going online: charged phone and power bank, mobile internet, weather-appropriate clothes, water.

Operator call after registration

After you submit the form, an operator will explain work and pay terms, give income figures, confirm your documents and transport type, and invite you to onboarding.

FAQ

How much does an X5 courier earn in Saint Petersburg?

At full load (7 days, 12 hours): on foot up to ~₽91,000 a month, e-bike up to ~₽145,000, car up to ~₽182,000. Plus goals and bonuses.

How do slots work?

Scheduled: booked in advance with fixed times. Free slot (exchange): no booking, inconvenient orders can be skipped with no rating loss; available to RF citizens.

When do payouts arrive?

Self-employed: every Thursday after 12:00 for the previous week, via T-Bank; backup Tuesday. GPH: every two weeks.

What goals and bonuses does X5 have?

Personal goals appear regularly: e.g. complete 240 orders by the deadline and earn ₽17,000. Amounts and terms depend on the city and month. Newcomers get a welcome bonus that varies by city. The rolling promo «Impuls» runs regularly — check the app for details.

Can I work via self-employment or a GPH contract while officially employed?

Yes. Self-employed status is compatible with official employment. A GPH contract via a park also works alongside a main job.

Can I work with a work patent?

Not in Saint Petersburg: patent holders are barred from courier work through 2026. EAEU citizens, RVP and VNZh holders work as usual — confirm your onboarding format with the operator.

What documents do I need to register?

RF citizens need a passport, INN and SNILS; online registration is possible. CIS citizens bring a passport and migration documents; onboarding may be online or at the courier center depending on the region.

Can I combine it with studies or another job?

Yes. The free slot (exchange) works as a side gig without booking. Scheduled slots can be taken in evenings and weekends — extra income for the officially employed.
